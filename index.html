Privacy Policy

Context Window (CCA cheat sheet) · Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Context Window mobile app ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by Yurii Malakhovskyi ("I", "me").

Summary

The app does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The only data processed is what is needed to handle the optional one-time purchase that unlocks all content.

Information the app collects

The app itself does not collect personal data. All study content is bundled inside the app and works offline.

Purchases

If you choose to buy the one-time unlock, two third parties process the purchase:

  • Google Play handles the payment. I never receive your name, email address or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat validates the purchase and lets you restore it on reinstall. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your device, purchase and transaction details (such as product ID, price, currency and purchase date), and basic technical information (such as device OS and version, app version, and approximate country derived from IP address). This ID is not linked to your name or email. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

This information is used only to confirm and restore your purchase.

Sharing

I do not sell or share your information with anyone other than the service providers listed above, and only for the purpose described.

Data retention and deletion

Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their own policies. To ask for the purchase data associated with your app user ID to be deleted from RevenueCat, email me at the address below.

Children

The app is a study aid for a professional certification and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
